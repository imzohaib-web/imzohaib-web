## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineering student** focused on engineering robust, responsive, and scalable digital products. My work bridges modern frontend architectures in **React** with resilient backend services in **Node.js** and **Express.js**, accompanied by an active focus on **AI integration** and data-driven systems.

- 🎓 **Academic Path**: Pursuing a degree in **Software Engineering**, emphasizing software design patterns, data structures, and distributed systems.
- 💻 **Full-Stack Craft**: Designing end-to-end applications with clean architectures, RESTful APIs, and secure database modeling (MongoDB & MySQL).
- 🤖 **AI & Intelligence**: Exploring practical AI integrations, LLM workflows, predictive models, and machine learning libraries (NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow).
- 🎯 **Engineering Principles**: Focused on maintainable code, test-driven reliability, intuitive UX/UI, and continuous improvement.
